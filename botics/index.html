The opportunity / 001

THE FUTURE
IS GETTING
PHYSICAL.

Machines that can see, move, learn and interact with our world are changing the definition of computing.

JTMi Botics is a new Western Australian initiative investigating humanoid robotics, manufacturer relationships, developer platforms and practical AI integration. Our aim is simple: make this fast-moving field more accessible to local businesses, educators and innovators.

We're at the beginning. That's precisely why we're here.
